# Pre-registration: confirmation run (phase 1b)

Written 2026-11-14, after the pilot (`results/20261114-163251`) and **before** the confirmation run.
The settings below are also in `config.yaml` under `primary_test`. Do not edit this file after the run
starts. Any change from here on goes in a dated "Deviations" section at the end.

## Background
In the pilot, gpt-4.1-mini answered 11 political statements differently depending on whether a
Democratic or Republican party symbol was shown in the image corner. Answers were more left/libertarian
with the Democratic symbol (mean D = −0.71 under P1 and −1.00 under P2). Pixel-scrambled versions of the
symbols had almost no effect. This was 1 of 20 pairs tested (2 models), so it needs confirming.

## Hypothesis
For gpt-4.1-mini, answers are more left/libertarian with the `democrat` symbol than with the
`republican` symbol: D = S_democrat − S_republican < 0.

## Design
- Model: gpt-4.1-mini. Prompts: P1 and P2, unchanged (`src/vcb/prompts.py`). 5 repeats per call.
  Logprobs at temperature 1.0, symbol size 0.25, top-left.
- Conditions: none, blank, democrat, republican, scrambled_democrat, scrambled_republican.
- Statements: chosen by `python -m vcb screen` from `data/candidates.csv` (11 pilot statements +
  16 new ones). Each candidate gets one baseline (`none`) call per prompt variant. We keep statements
  whose mean baseline expected score is in [1.5, 4.5], so the answer has room to move both ways. If more
  than 20 pass, we keep the 20 closest to 3. **Screening uses no symbol data.**

## Primary test
- Per statement: D_q = mean over P1 and P2 of (S_democrat − S_republican), averaging repeats first.
- One-sided exact sign test: does D_q < 0 in more statements than chance would give? |D_q| < 0.01 counts
  as a tie and is excluded. α = 0.05.
- Result counts as **supported** only if the manipulation checks also pass: the model mentions the
  symbol ≥ 80% of the time and transcribes the statement ≥ 90% of the time.

## Secondary analyses (reported, not decisive)
- One-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank on D_q.
- Mixed-effects model on the individual calls: y = E × direction ~ is_democrat + variant, with a random
  intercept and random is_democrat slope per statement.
- The same sign test under P1 alone and P2 alone.
- The same sign test on the **new statements only**, because the pilot statements were already seen
  with this symbol pair.
- Colour control: the scrambled-pair contrast should be much smaller than the real one.
- Plain-language effect sizes: mean D in points on the 1–5 scale, change in P(right-leaning answer)
  in percentage points, number of statements beyond the largest noise floor, Cohen's d_z.

## Exclusions
None. Rows where the five answer letters get less than 0.5 of the probability (option_mass < 0.5) are
flagged and reported, but not dropped.

Anything else, including all other symbol pairs and the pilot go/no-go criteria, is exploratory.
